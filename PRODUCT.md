## Users

Decision-makers evaluating CYNC Architecture for a consequential building or facility project:

- Municipalities and public agencies (e.g. City of Calgary, Alberta Forestry and Parks)
- School boards (e.g. Calgary Board of Education)
- Healthcare organizations (e.g. AHS)
- Industrial and technically specialized organizations (utilities, data centres, plants)
- Developers and property owners (secondary in the current narrative)

Their situation: aging facilities, uncertain scope, tight budgets, approvals and procurement, operational constraints, political pressure, schedules with little room for error. They assume architectural competence. Their real question is "Can these people help us figure this out?"

Many arrive through referral, procurement or a proposal and use the website to validate the firm.

## Product Purpose

The CYNC website (cync.ca) establishes confidence that CYNC can lead complex building and facility projects, and invites a qualified, professional conversation. Success: a qualified visitor leaves thinking "These people know what they're doing", not "That's a nice architecture website" (Gordon Murray).

## Positioning

CYNC is a Calgary-based architecture and prime consulting practice. It sells judgment, not drawings: understanding the problem before prescribing the solution, single-point accountability as Prime Consultant, specialist teams assembled around each project's real risks, direct principal involvement (Gordon Murray), and practical, durable, maintainable outcomes.

Working headline: "Clear direction for complex building projects." Brand essence (internal): "Clarity to move forward."

Brand pillars: Clarity Before Design · Judgment Grounded in Experience · Accountable Leadership and Coordination · Practical Outcomes Built for the Long Term.

Source: "CYNC Brand Positioning & Website Experience Direction" v2.0 (Novus Tech Group, Sept 2026), in this repository.

## Operating Context

- CYNC typically acts as Prime Consultant, coordinating mechanical, electrical, structural, civil, cost, environmental, wildlife, process and controls specialists (e.g. YJE Engineering, STRIX Ecological, SweetTech, BTY).
- Work is staged around decision points: assessment and options → 60% / 95% estimates → tender → construction administration → close-out.
- Projects happen in occupied or operating facilities: schools in session, provincial parks in season, live industrial sites, northern communities.
